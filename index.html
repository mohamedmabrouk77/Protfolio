01 ABOUT

Backend engineer building real-world systems

I'm a dedicated .NET Backend Engineer specialized in designing and building secure, scalable, and production-ready backend systems. My focus is on APIs and business logic that power complex platforms, especially in medical and enterprise systems.

I have hands-on experience developing large-scale systems such as Raaya Medical Platform, where I built core backend modules including authentication, booking workflows, hospital dashboards, pharmacy management, and role-based access using ASP.NET Core, EF Core, SQL Server, and JWT.

I strongly believe in Clean Architecture, clear separation of concerns, and writing maintainable code. I design APIs with performance, security, and real-world usage in mind — not just theoretical implementations.

Beyond backend development, I have a solid foundation in web fundamentals (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and experience integrating AI-powered services using Python for medical image analysis, letting me collaborate effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
